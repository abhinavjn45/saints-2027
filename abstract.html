Abstract submission

Abstracts are accepted until 25 November 2026 through the online submission form.

  • Abstracts due 25 November 2026
  • Acceptance by 30 November 2026
  • Conference 6–8 January 2027

Who can submit

Researchers, academicians, industry professionals, innovators and students are invited to submit abstracts presenting original and unpublished work aligned with the thematic tracks of SAINTS 2027.

SAINTS 2027 aims to foster interdisciplinary research collaborations and to provide a platform for exchanging knowledge and solutions to challenges in energy, water, health, environment, advanced materials, artificial intelligence, data science and sustainable development.

All abstracts must be prepared in the official SAINTS 2027 abstract template and submitted through the online submission form.

Presentation categories

SAINTS 2027 has two presentation formats: oral and poster.

Oral presentations

  • Presentation: 10 minutes
  • Discussion and Q&A: 5 minutes
  • Presentations must be delivered in English
  • Selected presentations may be considered for special awards

Poster presentations

  • Posters are displayed during dedicated poster sessions
  • Authors can interact with participants, experts and industry representatives
  • Poster dimensions and guidelines will be provided after acceptance

Presentation preference

When submitting, authors may indicate one of these preferences:

  • Oral presentation
  • Poster presentation
  • Either oral or poster

The Scientific Committee makes the final decision on presentation format, based on peer review and conference scheduling.

Abstract preparation

These requirements apply to every submission.

Requirements

  • Submit the abstract in Microsoft Word format (.doc or .docx)
  • Use the official SAINTS 2027 abstract template
  • Maximum length: one page
  • Clearly describe the background and objectives, methodology, key results, and major conclusions and their significance
  • Write in clear and concise English
  • Submissions must be original work that has not been published before

Publication

All accepted abstracts will be published in the SAINTS 2027 Book of Abstracts.

Book of Abstracts

The Book of Abstracts will

  • Be assigned an ISBN
  • Be made available to all conference participants
  • Serve as a permanent record of the scientific contributions presented at the conference

Selected high-quality papers may also be invited for submission to conference proceedings, edited books or special issues of partner journals, subject to peer review.

Conference awards

SAINTS 2027 will present the following awards.

Best Oral Presentation Award

For the most outstanding oral presentation, judged on:

  • Scientific quality
  • Originality and innovation
  • Technical merit
  • Clarity of presentation
  • Relevance to conference themes

Best Poster Presentation Award

For the most impactful poster, judged on:

  • Scientific excellence
  • Innovation
  • Visual presentation
  • Effective communication of research findings

Best Thesis Pitch Award

Recognises exceptional thesis work presented by postgraduate and doctoral researchers.

Innovation Award

Recognises innovative technologies, prototypes, start-ups or translational research with significant societal or industrial impact.
