About the conference

A meeting point for the sciences that move the world forward

The International Conference on Scientific Advances in Natural & Technological Sciences (SAINTS) is hosted by the School of Sciences at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bangalore. It brings together scholars across disciplines to present original research and build collaborations.

Over three days, delegates will attend keynote lectures, parallel technical sessions, poster presentations and workshops spanning four thematic tracks.
